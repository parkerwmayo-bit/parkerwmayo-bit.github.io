Diana privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Diana is a personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It has exactly one user, its owner, and is not available to anyone else.

Google data it uses

Gmail (send only). Diana can send an email from the owner's account only after the owner has reviewed and approved that exact message on their own computer. It cannot read, search or delete mail through this permission.

Google Calendar (its own calendar only). Diana can add events to a separate calendar that it creates. It cannot see or change any of the owner's other calendars. Invitations to other people are sent only after the owner approves them.

Where data goes

All data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. A log of what was sent is kept on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Diana's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Diana's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub profile.
